About SkyBridge

What we do

We book, consolidate, clear and deliver air freight. One team handles pickup, airline uplift, customs clearance and last-mile delivery.

Who we serve

Exporters, e-commerce sellers, pharma and medical suppliers, electronics makers and manufacturers who need spare parts on time.

Service area

India, Middle East, Southeast Asia, Europe and North America, with pickup across 40 Indian cities including Vadodara, Ahmedabad and Mumbai.

Why choose us

Live tracking, flat and transparent rates, 98% on-time delivery and a named account manager for every customer.

Services

Express Air

Door-to-door in 1–3 days. Priority uplift on the next available flight.

Standard Air

Economical airport-to-airport or door-to-door freight in 3–6 days.

Temperature-Controlled

Cold-chain handling for vaccines, medicines and perishables, with monitored loggers.

Customs Clearance

Documentation, duties and brokerage handled at origin and destination.

Dangerous Goods

Certified packing, labelling and airline approval for DG shipments.

Cargo Insurance

All-risk cover for loss and damage from pickup to delivery.
